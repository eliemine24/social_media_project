**Main aidea, to re-organise and re-write**

Recent analysis (2025 - ref to find (ask Robin Jenny)) found that from now, more than half of the traffic on internet is generated by non-human actors. This phenomenon can be observed mainly on social medias, were it affects significanty our use of these services, and our behavior online. One will even argue that AI-content generation, as much as bots, has major consequences on "real-life" events as nationala elections, that its influence on a large part of our life could not be neglected. 

For any user of social media, it is impossible not to see that Ai-generated content has taken a large part of the overall content we can watch online. it becomes difficult to spend some time scrolling without finding any Ai-generated picture, video, off-voice, or even background music. Consedering the large amount of produced content and the ubiquity of social media, it is relevant to investigate about the purposes of such content, and what it is used for. To achieve this, we will try to classify a large amount of AI-generated content in different categrories in order to identify the aim of ai generated content.

## Classification startegy

The first strategy was to manage to code a fully automated program to sort posts based on it's hashtags, but it early became obvious that this method wouldn't lead to relevant classification, taken into account many factors : 

- content self classification problem (Real agenda may be different than publicaly showed intentions),
- lack of accuracy of hashtags, which are not always mentionned in the posts, sometimes false, or biased, etc
- major presence of sarcasm and irony on social media, *and maybe especially in french culture*, which cannot be understood by such algorithm

To face this issue, we decided to use a quiet simple but efficient method : ask humans to sort the content themselves. *give arguments for such decision* 



# The App

please note that this readme is temporary.

We want to make a **web-app** that fetches AI-content on social media (instagram for AI referencing), and show it to you on a nice looking interface. From this interface/page and by seeing the content, you can classify it in pre-existent categories by clicking on buttons and then confirming your selection (this allows you to put the content in more than 1 category). Once you click on, say, "validate", the back-end update a database referencing what post goes in what categories, and immediatly skips to the next post to classify. We will use React for developping.
